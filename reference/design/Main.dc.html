Straight answers

Questions owners ask

Will callers know it's not a person?+

If they ask, it tells them. Most don't. They want their question answered and their message passed on, and that's what it does, in a calm, natural voice.

Can I keep my phone number?+

Yes. You forward your existing number to your receptionist, all the time or only when you don't pick up. Customers dial what they always have.

What happens if I go over 250 minutes?+

Extra minutes are [$0.35] each. We email you at 80% of your allowance, so there are no surprises. Website chat never uses minutes.

Why do text alerts take longer to switch on?+

US carriers require every business that sends texts to register first. Approval usually takes one to three weeks. Email summaries work from your first day.

Can it book appointments?+

Not on this plan. It takes the details and sends them to you straight away. Calendar booking, text-back to missed callers and review requests come with our done-for-you plan.

Is there a contract or setup fee?+

No to both. $97 a month, month to month. Cancel any time and forward your calls back.

How do I change what it says?+

New hours, a new service, a holiday closure: send us the change and we update it. No re-setup, no charge.
