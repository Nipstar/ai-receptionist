Voice: we say

  • "Every call answered." Outcomes, in the owner's words.
  • "You already paid for that call." The cost of the problem, plainly.
  • "It won't book into your calendar." Honest limits, up front.
  • $97, 250 minutes, no setup fee. Real numbers, always.

We don't say

  • AI-powered, cutting-edge, revolutionize, seamless, solution.
  • Invented stats or guaranteed results.
  • Robots, brains, glowing circuits, stock photos of headsets.
  • Exclamation marks. Pressure. "Limited time" we don't mean.
